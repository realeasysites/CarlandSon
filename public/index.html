Questions

Good to know before you call.

Do you do residential jobs too, or only commercial?

Both. Commercial flooring is our specialty, but we regularly do basements, rentals, kitchens and whole-home installs.

What areas do you cover?

We're based in Naples, Maine and serve all of New England — Maine, New Hampshire, Vermont, Massachusetts, Rhode Island and Connecticut. Larger commercial jobs anywhere in the region are welcome.

Can you work around my business hours?

Tell us your business hours when you reach out — we'll work with you on a schedule that keeps disruption to a minimum.

Are estimates really free?

Yes. We'll walk the space, measure, and give you a written quote with no obligation.

Do you supply the flooring or just install?

Either way. We can source materials for you or install flooring you've already purchased.
